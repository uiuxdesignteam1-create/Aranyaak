Welcome to Aranyaak

Private pools, green hills and time to yourself

Aranyaak is on Lawachara Road in Sreemangal, in the heart of Bangladesh's tea country. Seven cottages, each with its own swimming pool, an infinity terrace and its own view of the nature around it. Built for privacy and quiet.
